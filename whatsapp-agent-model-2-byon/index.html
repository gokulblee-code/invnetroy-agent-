1 Do I have to pay any monthly fees, cloud servers, or software subscription?

No. This agent runs with zero recurring software fees. Meta provides 1,000 free supplier WhatsApp conversations every month. Google AI Studio gives a free forever AI brain key. You can use this for your store completely free of ongoing SaaS costs.

2 What is the quickest way to restock today without setting up any Meta keys?

Use 1-Click WhatsApp! Just click the green button "🤖 Auto-Restock via WhatsApp" at the top of the screen. The AI instantly checks your store shelves, picks the best wholesale vendor from vendors.csv, and opens WhatsApp Web or your phone app with the discounted order pre-typed. You review it and hit send with 1 click.

3 How do I connect hands-free 24/7 WhatsApp so it negotiates while I sleep?

It takes only 5 minutes in Meta:

  1. Go to developers.facebook.com and click Create App > Business.
  2. Under products, click WhatsApp > Set up.
  3. Copy your Phone Number ID (e.g., 100609346426845).
  4. In Meta Business Suite Settings > System Users, click Generate Token with whatsapp_business_messaging.
  5. Click "⚙️ Settings & Tools ▾ > Store & WhatsApp Setup" in this app and paste them in!

4 What is a "Webhook" and why does WhatsApp need it?

Think of the Webhook as a store mailbox. When a wholesale supplier texts you back on WhatsApp (e.g. "We can do ₹260 per unit"), Meta delivers their text message to this mailbox so your AI assistant can read it, negotiate a better deal, and create your Purchase Order automatically.

5 How does the AI bargain with wholesale suppliers?

The AI references your past store procurement history and uses established commercial buying strategies: volume bundling, offering guaranteed 24-hour instant UPI settlement, and calculated Ackerman price concessions to guide the supplier down to your target wholesale price.

6 Can the AI accidentally spend all my store money?

Never! You set an Auto-Approval Spending Cap (default is ₹25,000). Any order below this amount is confirmed automatically. Any order above this threshold is immediately placed on HOLD and requires your manual manager approval or PIN before any payment link or PO is cleared.

7 Where do I update my supplier contacts and wholesale product quotes?

Click "⚙️ Settings & Tools" in the top right and select "📁 Wholesale Vendors Directory (CSV)". You can view, search, upload a new CSV, or edit phone numbers and product quotes directly from the browser.

8 What happens if the store internet goes down?

The agent features an automatic local failover engine. If cloud AI or internet connection drops, it instantly switches to its offline mathematical pricing matrix and continues monitoring store stock from the local POS SQLite database with 0% downtime.

How The Bot Decides When Two Suppliers Offer The Same Price

When two wholesale suppliers offer the exact same price (for example, both offer ₹250 per plant), the bot uses these 6 clear rules to pick the winner fairly:

1. On-Time Delivery 35% Importance

Past track record. Suppliers who deliver on time with zero damaged goods score highest.

2. Fast Speed & Distance 25% Importance

Nearby suppliers who can deliver in 24 hours beat suppliers who take 2 to 3 days.

3. Loyal Regular Supplier 20% Importance

Trusted long-term partners who have consistently supplied your store in the past get priority.

4. Easy UPI Payment 10% Importance

Suppliers who accept instant direct UPI payment without extra surcharges or hassles.

5. Split Big Orders Stock Safety

For large orders (40+ items), the order is split 60%/40% so your store never runs out if one is delayed.

6. Take Turns 100% Fair

If both suppliers are equal on all points, the order goes to the supplier who did not get the last order.
