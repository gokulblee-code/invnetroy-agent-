# Book C8: Artisanal Planters: Terracotta vs Ceramic Selection
## Catalog Options
- Traditional Terracotta: ₹160 (Porous clay promotes natural root aeration, prevents root rot).
- Glazed Nordic Ceramic Pots: ₹290 – ₹340 (Minimalist matte finish in white, anthracite, and sage green).

## Selection Guidelines
- Moisture-loving plants (Peace Lily): Glazed Ceramic retains steady hydration.
- Succulents and low-water plants (Snake, Jade): Porous Terracotta allows rapid drying.
