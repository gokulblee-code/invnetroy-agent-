# Book C11: IT Professional Workspace & Desk Greenery
## Problem Statement
Software developers, coders, and desk workers spend 8–12 hours facing monitors in sealed air-conditioned cabins, leading to dry eyes, screen fatigue, and VOC exposure.

## Recommended Solution
- Desk Greenery Duo: Snake Plant (₹220) + ZZ Plant (₹280).
- Zero Care Required: Survives missed waterings during heavy project release sprints.
- Visual Relief: Natural emerald green foliage reduces eye strain.
