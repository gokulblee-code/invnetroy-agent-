# Book C5: Monstera Deliciosa Living Lounge Styling Guide
## Specimen Specifications
- Name: Monstera Deliciosa (Swiss Cheese Plant)
- Pricing: ₹499 (Large Architectural Specimen)
- Aesthetic: Dramatic split fenestrated leaves; the ultimate statement centerpiece for living rooms and foyers.

## Maintenance & Leaf Hygiene
- Cleaning: Wipe leaves monthly with a soft damp cotton cloth to remove dust and maximize photosynthesis.
- Watering: Water every 8–10 days. Provide moss pole support for vertical growth.
