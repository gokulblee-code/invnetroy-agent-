# Book C4: Peace Lily Blooming & Humidity Care Sheet
## Specimen Specifications
- Name: Peace Lily (Spathiphyllum Wallisii)
- Pricing: ₹340 (Standard) | ₹380 (Specimen Bloom Grade)
- Blooms: Pure white spathe flower blooms that add serene elegance to living spaces.

## Care & Dynamic Signaling
- Communication Trait: Leaves visibly droop when thirsty, serving as a natural watering alert, then rebound within 1 hour.
- Watering: Water once every 6–8 days. Keep soil evenly moist but not waterlogged.
- Light: Bright indirect sunlight. Protect from direct harsh noon sun.
