# Book C3: ZZ Plant Low-Light & AC Office Resilience Guide
## Specimen Specifications
- Name: ZZ Plant (Zamioculcas Zamiifolia)
- Pricing: ₹280 (Standard Nursery Pot) | ₹520 (Textured Ceramic Pot)
- Foliage: Glossy, waxy emerald-green leaves that relieve ocular fatigue.

## Resilience Characteristics
- Drought Resistance: Subterranean potato-like rhizomes store moisture for weeks.
- Environment: Survives in dark, windowless cubicles and dry, drafty air-conditioned software workspaces.
- Watering: Water once every 20–25 days. Overwatering is the only way to harm this plant.
