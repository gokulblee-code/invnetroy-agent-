# Book C9: Kerala Monsoon vs Summer Watering Calendars
## Climate Tuning for Coastal Kerala
- Monsoon Season (June – November): High ambient humidity (85%+). Soil evaporation slows dramatically.
  - Snake / ZZ: Water once every 18–22 days.
  - Foliage / Palms: Water once every 10–12 days.
- Summer Season (December – May): High heat (32°C–36°C) and rapid evaporation.
  - Snake / ZZ: Water once every 10–14 days.
  - Foliage / Palms: Water every 5–7 days.
