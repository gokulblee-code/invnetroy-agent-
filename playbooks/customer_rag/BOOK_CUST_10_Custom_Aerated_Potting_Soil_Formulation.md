# Book C10: Custom Aerated Potting Soil Formulation
## Merlot Soil Recipe
- 40% Low-EC Washed Coco-Peat (Moisture retention without clumping).
- 30% Horticultural Perlite (Prevents soil compaction and oxygenates root zone).
- 20% Aged Organic Vermicompost (Continuous slow-release bio-nutrients).
- 10% Crushed Neem Seed Cake (Natural systemic protection against soil gnats and fungus).
