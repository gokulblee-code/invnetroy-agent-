# Book C2: Snake Plant Complete Care & Air Purification Guide
## Specimen Specifications
- Name: Snake Plant (Sansevieria Trifasciata / Laurentii / Golden Hahnii)
- Pricing: ₹220 (Grow Bag) | ₹450 (Glazed Nordic Ceramic Pot)
- Air Purification: Ranked #1 in NASA Clean Air Study. Filters benzene, formaldehyde, xylene, and electronic VOCs.
- Nocturnal Oxygen: Performs CAM photosynthesis, emitting fresh oxygen at night during sleep hours.

## Watering & Maintenance
- Watering Schedule: Water once every 14–20 days. Allow top 2 inches of soil to completely dry out.
- Light Conditions: Highly adaptable; thrives in low light, artificial office LED lighting, or bright indirect sun.
- Ideal Placement: Bedrooms, study desks, and computer setups.
