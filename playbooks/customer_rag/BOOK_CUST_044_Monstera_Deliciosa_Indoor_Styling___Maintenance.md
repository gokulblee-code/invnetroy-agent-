### Chapter 1: Botanical Overview & Species Profile
Fenestrated Swiss cheese leaves adding dramatic architectural presence to living rooms.
- **Optimal Placement:** Bright indirect sunlight, 3 to 6 feet from east or north-facing windows.
- **Watering Discipline:** Allow top 50% of potting mix to dry out completely before deep soaking.
- **Soil Formulation:** Coarse, free-draining blend of 50% coco peat, 25% perlite, and 25% vermicompost.

### Chapter 2: Seasonal Climate & Kerala Monsoon Guidelines
- **High Humidity (Monsoon):** In high-humidity coastal climates (80-95% RH), reduce watering frequency by half. Ensure drain holes are unobstructed.
- **Air-Conditioned Offices:** Keep plant away from direct AC cold draft vents; wipe glossy foliage monthly with a damp cloth to clear dust pores.
- **Pet Safety Notice:** Classified according to ASPCA non-toxic standards or high-oxalate warnings. Always keep delicate foliage away from teething pets.
