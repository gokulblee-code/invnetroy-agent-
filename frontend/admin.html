📖 Recommended Negotiation Literature for Ingestion

📕

"Never Split the Difference: Negotiating As If Your Life Depended On It" — Chris Voss

Core Playbook: Tactical empathy, calibrated "how/what" questions, Ackerman bargaining step formulas (65% → 85% → 95% → 100%), and non-monetary concession buffers (e.g. instant corporate UPI payment for price concession).

📗

"Getting to Yes: Negotiating Agreement Without Giving In" — Roger Fisher & William Ury

Core Playbook: Principled negotiation, separating the problem from the vendor relationship, establishing an objective BATNA (Best Alternative to a Negotiated Agreement), and mutual-gain trade-offs.

📘

"Bargaining for Advantage: Negotiation Strategies for Reasonable People" — G. Richard Shell

Core Playbook: Information-based bargaining, supplier leverage matrices, anchoring psychological price expectations, and closing strategies for high-frequency wholesale reorders.

📙

"The Negotiation Fieldbook: Simple Strategies to Help You Negotiate Anything" — Grande Lum

Core Playbook: Concrete B2B supply-chain contracts, rejecting ancillary transport charges, volume bundling, and delivery penalty clauses.
